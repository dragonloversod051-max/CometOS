Relseiðrs

Relseiðrs are, to be simple, elements. They are elements that go beyond, far beyond the 118 known to mankind. The reason they are so set apart is because relseiðrs have magical properties.

They are imbued with Arcane energy, and are therefore used in a multitude of functions, ranging from spellcasting to being used in Cloudboards, or in enhanced items. Some famous relseiðrs which nearly every member of Alarica has heard about include Xarenium, Vylium, Mantica, and Asteria.

Relseiðrs also react and combine differently to normal elements. The nature and laws however, are not entirely known, though we have still managed to lay aside a few theorums and certainties about how certain relseiðrs will combine in specific quantities.

To start, Relseiðrs are divided into classes. Each class is given a ranking, ranging from Common, to Rare, to Exotic, to Legendary. (And of course, there are about a hundred subdivisions as well. These are Relseiðrs, after all). Classes ranked Common through Rare always produce more material than it started with. Classes ranked higher produce less material. And there are reactions where you can interchange rarites and classes, and a whole bunch of other things. Essentially, these mystical elements behave as wildly as their power does.
